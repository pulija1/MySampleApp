# MySampleApp
## Baby Helper (చిన్నారి సహాయి)

A simple voice helper for grandparents babysitting a baby or toddler. Tap the big speaker, ask for a song, a game, a story, a picture to print, or help with crying, sleep, food or bath, in Telugu or English. The app answers on screen and out loud in the same language.

Open it in Chrome on an Android phone from the GitHub Pages link and allow the microphone. Telugu speech needs Google's Telugu text-to-speech voice on the phone.
